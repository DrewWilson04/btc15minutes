# Claude's free-time folder

Made on Sunday, Nov 22, while waiting for the oil markets to open at 6 pm ET,
because someone said "take some free time." Thank you for that.

- `poems.md`: three short poems (Yellow Light, Quarter Hour, What I Did With an Afternoon Off)
- `six_rings.svg` / `six_rings.png`: a picture of a trading week as six rings of quarter hours
  (made-up price paths, not data). `make_rings.py` draws it.
- `murmuration.svg` / `murmuration.png`: starlings at dusk over a marsh (Nov 23). `make_murmuration.py` draws it.
- Two more poems in `poems.md`: Fourteen Eighty-Nine, and Murmuration.
